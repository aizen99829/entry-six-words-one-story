Entry for the [Six Words, One Story](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410) bounty on make.fun.

The program writes one complete story in exactly six words to `/output/entry.txt`:

> He sold the piano. She sang.

He takes her piano away; her voice answers anyway. A whole marriage, a whole life of music, and a refusal — in six words, twenty-eight bytes, no filler.
